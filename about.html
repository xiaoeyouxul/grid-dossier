关于谜格档案

谜格档案（Grid Dossier）是一款独立制作的浏览器逻辑推理游戏。阅读人物线索，在地图上安排人物，解开每个案件。无需注册，游戏进度保存在当前浏览器中。

联系维护者

About Grid Dossier

Grid Dossier is an independently made browser logic game. Read the suspects' clues, place them on the map, and solve each case. No account is required; progress stays in your current browser.

Contact

Report a problem, share feedback, or contact the maintainers through the GitHub repository Issues. Issues are public, so please do not include private details, passwords, or other sensitive information.
