隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 29 日

我们收集和保存什么

谜格档案目前是一款静态浏览器游戏，不要求注册，也没有本站运营的账户、服务器或分析服务。游戏进度（包括棋盘、标记、计时和完成状态）、外观、语言及音乐偏好保存在你当前浏览器的本地存储（localStorage）中，用于恢复游戏体验；这些数据不会由本游戏同步到其他设备。清除浏览器网站数据会删除它们。

背景音乐是可选的，由浏览器 Web Audio API 实时生成。音乐开关偏好保存在本地；本站不会因此访问麦克风或上传音频。

网站托管在 GitHub Pages。访问网页时，GitHub 可能按照其自身隐私政策处理技术请求数据。请参阅 GitHub 隐私声明。

广告与 Cookie

截至本政策更新之日，本网站没有展示广告，也没有安装 Google AdSense 广告代码，因此目前不会通过本站投放 Google 个性化广告。

如果未来启用 Google AdSense，我们会先更新本政策并在适用情况下提供所需的隐私选择。届时 Google 及其合作伙伴可能使用 Cookie 或类似技术，根据你的访问记录展示、衡量或个性化广告；广告个性化选项可在 Google 广告设置管理。有关 Google 如何使用网站或应用数据，请参阅 Google 合作伙伴网站数据使用说明及 Google 隐私权政策。以上仅说明未来可能的情况，目前本站尚未启用这些广告技术。

你的选择与联系

你可以在浏览器设置中清除本网站的本地存储。关于隐私或本政策的问题，请通过 GitHub 仓库的 Issues联系维护者；请勿在公开问题中提交密码或其他敏感信息。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Information and local storage

Grid Dossier is currently a static browser game. It does not require an account, and we do not operate a game backend or analytics service. Game progress (including board placements, marks, timer, and completion state), appearance, language, and music preferences are stored in localStorage in your current browser so the game can restore your session. The game does not sync this data across devices. Clearing this site's browser data removes it.

Background music is optional and generated in your browser with the Web Audio API. The music preference is stored locally. The site does not access your microphone or upload audio.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data when you visit, under its own privacy statement. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Advertising and cookies

As of this policy's date, this site displays no ads and has no Google AdSense code installed. It therefore does not currently serve Google personalized ads.

If Google AdSense is enabled in the future, we will update this policy and provide privacy choices where required. Google and its partners may then use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, or personalize ads based on visits to this and other sites. You can manage ad personalization in Google Ads Settings. Learn more about how Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. This describes a possible future setup; those ad technologies are not currently enabled here.

Your choices and contact

You can remove this site's local storage through your browser settings. For privacy questions or policy feedback, contact the maintainers through the GitHub repository Issues. Please do not post passwords or other sensitive information in a public issue.
