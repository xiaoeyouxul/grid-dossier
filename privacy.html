隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 9 月 30 日

我们收集和保存什么

谜格档案目前是一款静态浏览器游戏，不要求注册，也没有本站运营的账户、服务器或分析服务。游戏进度（包括棋盘、标记、计时和完成状态）、外观、语言及音乐偏好保存在你当前浏览器的本地存储（localStorage）中，用于恢复游戏体验；这些数据不会由本游戏同步到其他设备。清除浏览器网站数据会删除它们。

背景音乐是可选的，由浏览器 Web Audio API 实时生成。音乐开关偏好保存在本地；本站不会因此访问麦克风或上传音频。

网站托管在 Cloudflare Pages 和 GitHub Pages。访问相应网址时，托管服务商可能按照其自身隐私政策处理技术请求数据。请参阅 Cloudflare 隐私政策和 GitHub 隐私声明。

广告与 Cookie

本网站已加入 Google AdSense 代码以验证网站并申请广告审核；在 Google 批准前，本站不展示 AdSense 广告。该代码由 Google 提供，加载时 Google 可能处理技术请求数据。获批并投放广告后，Google 及其合作伙伴可能使用 Cookie 或类似技术展示、衡量或个性化广告。你可以在 Google 广告设置管理广告个性化。详情请参阅 Google 合作伙伴网站数据使用说明及 Google 隐私权政策。

对于适用地区，本站会依照相关要求提供广告隐私选择。AdSense 审核和相关隐私设置完成前，广告不会正式投放。

你的选择与联系

你可以在浏览器设置中清除本网站的本地存储。关于隐私或本政策的问题，请通过 GitHub 仓库的 Issues联系维护者；请勿在公开问题中提交密码或其他敏感信息。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Information and local storage

Grid Dossier is currently a static browser game. It does not require an account, and we do not operate a game backend or analytics service. Game progress (including board placements, marks, timer, and completion state), appearance, language, and music preferences are stored in localStorage in your current browser so the game can restore your session. The game does not sync this data across devices. Clearing this site's browser data removes it.

Background music is optional and generated in your browser with the Web Audio API. The music preference is stored locally. The site does not access your microphone or upload audio.

The site is hosted by Cloudflare Pages and GitHub Pages. The host serving the address you visit may process technical request data under its own privacy policy. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement.

Advertising and cookies

This site includes Google AdSense code for site verification and ad review. It does not display AdSense ads before Google approves the site. Google provides this code and may process technical request data when it loads. After approval, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, or personalize ads. You can manage ad personalization in Google Ads Settings. See how Google uses data from partner sites and the Google Privacy Policy.

We will provide advertising privacy choices where required. Ads will not be served until AdSense review and applicable privacy settings are complete.

Your choices and contact

You can remove this site's local storage through your browser settings. For privacy questions or policy feedback, contact the maintainers through the GitHub repository Issues. Please do not post passwords or other sensitive information in a public issue.
